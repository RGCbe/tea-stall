# Tea stall plan

Public path only. No adult scenes on these pages, on WEBTOON, or on the Tea Break channel.

Order: draw the manga, upload it, post a review video, then animate the same pages.

## Budget

₹2,000 a month on GridShare is about 110 hours of an RTX 3060 12 GB after GST. Episode 1 uses a few of those hours. Stop the machine when the files are copied off.

Model for the pages: Illustrious XL. Draw one clean reference of the tea seller first, and reuse it so the face stays the same. Lettering is added afterward in our own code. The model does not paint words.

## 1. Manga

One episode, about 24 panels, vertical.

- Each panel is drawn at 832×1216, then saved as 800×1280 JPG, under 2 MB.
- Panel 1 is the tea stall at night and the seller. The middle panels are one small problem at the stall. The last panel is the cup and the hook for episode 2.
- No swords in episode 1. A fight can wait until the face and the stall are stable.
- Name the files 01.jpg, 02.jpg, in order.

## 2. Manga site

WEBTOON Canvas, on a computer.

- Series title, a 2–3 sentence summary, a 1080×1080 thumbnail, and a 1080×1920 thumbnail.
- Episode title, for example “Episode 1 – The Tea Stall”.
- Episode thumbnail 160×151.
- Upload 01.jpg onward in order. Preview, then publish.
- Creator note: “Story and lettering by Manoj. Art made with AI tools.”

## 3. Review video

Made from the stills. No extra GPU. The pictures glide panel to panel, with a voice track.

- Length about 4–6 minutes.
- Say what happens, then one opinion, then the WEBTOON link in the description and the pinned comment.
- Mark it as AI art.
- Publish on the Tea Break channel the same day as the episode, or the day after. Do not use the adult account.

## 4. Animation

Only after the review is up. Animate the same 24 panels. Do not invent a new film.

- On the 3060, each shot is about 3–4 seconds. Twenty-four shots are about 90 seconds.
- Expect several bad takes. Budget a few hours, then stop the GPU.
- Cut the good clips in order, keep the same voice, and put the WEBTOON link in the description.
- A smooth 3-minute film needs the RTX 3090 and a later test. Episode 1 does not wait for that.

## Done when

The Canvas episode is public, the review is on YouTube with the link, and the 90-second animation uses only panels from that episode.
